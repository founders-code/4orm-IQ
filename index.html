What a result is allowed to say

Silence is never clearance.

Most checks answer with a tick. A tick cannot tell the difference between a source that was searched and held nothing, and a source that could not be reached at all. 4ormIQ writes which one happened, in words, every time.

Official record located

One authoritative record exists, and here it is.

It does not mean anything broader than that one record.

Source reached, no result

The search completed and the source held nothing.

It does not mean risk or misconduct is absent.

Source unavailable

The retrieval did not complete, and we say so.

It does not mean the check came back clean.

Conflict or stale

Sources disagree, or the currency needs review.

It does not give us permission to show you the convenient one.

4ormfinance.com Privacy Compliance

4ormIQ is published by 4orm Finance. It reports what named public sources hold and what they do not, with the date and scope of each. It is not a credit check, a background check, a legal opinion or financial advice, and it does not clear, rate or recommend any company. A source we could not reach is reported as unreached, never as clear.
