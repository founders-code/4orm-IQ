03

Findings

Major

The action sits behind the evidence, and on a phone it sits four screens down

The result screen offers one way forward: What we found. From there, a second press reaches Do this right now. There is no route from the verdict to the action. A reader who has just read Do not send anything tonight is two presses and a screen of evidence away from the only page that tells them to ring their bank.

On a phone it is worse than two presses. The way on sits at 3.96 screenfuls down a page that runs 4.66 screenfuls. On desktop it sits at 1.82, still below the fold.

Measured: rpToFound top offset / viewport height, 390x844 and 1440x1000, finished report, build 20260917.1811
The fix

Put the action on the result screen. Where the verdict is adverse, or the reader has said money has gone, the result screen offers Do this right now beside What we found, and it sits with the verdict rather than at the foot. One press from what happened to what to do.

Major

Sources and method is a dead end

The screen that explains how a record is found and read has exactly one way off it, and it points backwards. A reader arrives there to satisfy themselves the work is sound, which is the moment they are most ready to act, and the product offers them nothing but the way they came.

Measured: every visible control on rpSources. One exit, rpBackToReport
The fix

A way on, not only a way back. Sources carries the same forward control the other screens carry, so a convinced reader goes to what to do rather than retracing.

Minor, but it compounds

One destination, three labels

How we decide sits in the foot of three screens. Sources and method sits in the header pill of the act screen. Both open the same page, and its own sections are titled a third way. A reader pays the recognition cost three times and cannot tell whether they have already been there.

Measured: control labels on rpReport, rpFound, rpAct, rpSources
The fix

One name for one destination, everywhere it appears: How we decide.

Minor

Check another name exists on one screen out of four

New check is on the result screen only. It is the most repeated action in the product, and from what we found, what to do or sources there is no way to start another one without walking back.

Measured: rpNewCheck present on rpReport, absent on rpFound, rpAct, rpSources
The fix

New check in the foot of every report screen, in the small print row that already carries the standing documents.

Minor

The waiting screen keeps announcing a result that has already been shown

waitPhase is an aria live region. After the sweep finishes and the screen closes, it is left holding Assessment ready in 3s. A screen reader announces a status about a screen nobody is on. The house standard says a live region is cleared when it stops being true.

Measured: aria-live content after waitClose, build 20260917.1811
The fix

Empty it in waitClose.

Minor

The things a reader acts on cannot be addressed

Rows 02 and 03 on the act screen, and all four findings on what we found, carry no id. They cannot be linked to, opened from another screen, or measured individually. Every navigation improvement above needs at least one of them to be addressable.

Measured: visible controls with no id, rpFound and rpAct
The fix

An id on each, named for what it is rather than where it sits.

Minor

Five ways off a screen that offers three things to do

The act screen carries back to what we found, back to the result and sources and method in its header, and how we decide and the data room in its foot. The three things it exists for compete with five ways to leave.

Measured: visible controls on rpAct outside the three rows
The fix

One way back, one way on, and the standing links in the foot where they already live.

04

What to change, in the order it pays

  1. Put Do this right now on the result screen. Beside the verdict, not at the foot, and only where the verdict is adverse or the reader has said money has gone. This is the whole finding above, and it is one change.
  2. Pin the next action on a phone. Once the reader passes the first screenful of the result, a single bar follows them carrying the one thing to do next. Nothing else in it.
  3. Give sources a way on. The same forward control the other screens carry.
  4. One label for one destination. How we decide, in every place that opens it.
  5. New check in the foot of every report screen.
  6. Clear the live region when the waiting screen closes.
  7. Id every row and every finding, which is what makes one, two and three buildable and measurable.
  8. Thin the act header to one way back.
05

What could not be confirmed

Three things, named rather than left silent.

Live retrieval was not exercised. Every measurement here ran against the seeded specimen. The live path shares the same rendering code, and the register health writing was corrected this week, but no live run was measured for this document.

Nobody outside the build has used it. Every number here is a machine measurement or my own reading. That is a heuristic walk, and a heuristic walk is not a participant study. Until somebody who has not seen it before runs a check without being shown around, comprehension is unverified.

Two control documents still disagree with the board. SEC-001 and VEND-001 carry NO-GO on their own covers while the release board records them amber. The build names it on every run. It is a decision for the owner, not a defect this audit can close.
