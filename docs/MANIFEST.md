## The evidence
FCAC, Exploring consumer behaviour in mortgage renewal, March 2026: 13 per cent of
mortgage holders did not know negotiating was possible, 20 per cent compared no lenders,
37 per cent chose their lender because they already banked there.

Do not claim more than that. FCAC publishes no data on negotiation outcomes, savings
achieved, or how often people accept the first offer.
