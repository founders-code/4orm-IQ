# Request for permission to link to and describe the OSC investor alerts

**To** Ontario Securities Commission
**From** 4orm Finance, Calgary, Alberta
**Date** 19 September 2026
**Subject** Permission under the OSC website terms of use, 4orm IQ

Dear Sir or Madam,

4orm Finance operates 4orm IQ at 4ormiq.com. It is a free consumer tool. A member of the
public types the name or website of a company they are about to send money to, and we
report what public registers say about it, with the source and the date beside every
line, and with what we could not reach printed alongside. There is no account, no fee,
and no advertising. We take no payment from any party we report on, and no entry can be
removed for money.

We read the OSC investor alerts and warnings. We are writing because your terms of use bar site content
from being put into any database or into any product or service offered for or without
fee, and we would rather ask than assume that a free tool sits outside that.

**What we do today.** Where the alerts page carries an entry for a company a reader has
asked about, the result says so in our own words, gives the date we read it, and links to
your page. We do not reproduce your text, we do not store your entry, and we do not
quote it. The reader goes to you to read what you published.

**What we are asking for.** Written confirmation that pointing a reader at the Caution
List, and stating in our own words that it carries an entry on a given date, is permitted
under your terms. If it is not, please tell us and we will remove the source.

**What we will do either way.** If we do not hear from you, or if the answer is no, we
will keep the source as link and label only, as it is now, or drop it entirely on your
instruction.

We would be glad to show you the tool, the source catalogue and the retrieval log for any
check.

Yours faithfully,

Chad Johnston
Founder, 4orm Finance
compliance@4ormiq.com
