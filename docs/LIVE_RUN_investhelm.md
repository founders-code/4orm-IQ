## BEFORE YOU SEND - DO THESE THREE THINGS

**1 · Confirm the warning yourself. Two minutes.**
Go to `bcsc.bc.ca`, open the Investment Caution List, and search Investhelm. It is
there, added 14 July 2026. You do not have to take our word for any of this.

**2 · Ask them three questions in writing.**
What is your legal entity name and registration number? Which regulator authorises you,
and what is your licence number? What name will appear on the receiving account? A real
firm answers all three in one email. Refusal, deflection, or a request to move to
WhatsApp is your answer.

**3 · If you have already paid, move today.**
Credit card and PayPal both have dispute windows and they close. Call your card issuer
or open a PayPal dispute now. Bank transfer and crypto have no recovery path - if that
is how you paid, report it to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre and your bank immediately.
